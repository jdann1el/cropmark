# Rubric (1-5 each, be strict: 3 = competent template, 4 = good studio work, 5 = portfolio)
1. Hook at feed size — reads at 320/360 px in under a second.
2. Understood without reading — the image carries the idea.
3. Single focus — one hero, clear reading order.
4. Typography — deliberate pairing, contrast of size/weight, spacing, accents.
5. Hierarchy & air — margins, quiet zones, nothing cramped or empty without reason.
6. Niche/brand fit — looks like the best account in that niche.
7. Finish — no artefacts, no clipping, no invented data, consistent set.
Zero-text pieces: score Typography as n/a and average the rest (the hook criterion then judges the image).
Stop rule: initial result plus at most two refinement passes for the whole piece/set. Record each failed gate, observed defect and proposed correction. If a feasible permitted correction exists and a refinement remains, perform that refinement and inspect/rescore the full set before delivery. Stop early only for a concrete asset/tool/permission blocker or a user stop, recording why remaining rounds cannot resolve it. If a delivery gate remains unmet at the limit, report it and deliver as unresolved rather than approved.
Ship ≥4.0 average with no criterion <3. Portfolio target: ≥4.3.
Score against the style card's reference level, not against the baseline.

Delivery additionally requires legibility (criterion 1) and finish (criterion 7) ≥4, complete required content, and no severe or visible technical defect. For faithful copies, score fidelity separately (≥4): accurate layout, typography, colour, identity and copy; originality is not a substitute. Record the evidence and any n/a, not just an average.
