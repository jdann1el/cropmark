# Critique by task, from renders

Use 1–5 as discussion anchors: 1 broken, 2 weak, 3 competent/template, 4 convincing studio work, 5 exceptional portfolio work. For each rating cite a visible observation; a high self-score cannot end review by itself.

Shared criteria: message/content fidelity; focus and composition; typography; image craft (n/a when absent); specificity of concept (n/a for faithful copy); finish. Judge readability at feed size and craft at full size.

Format lenses:
- Thumbnail: immediately visible story/stakes, recognition, hook and truthful relationship to the video; preserve identifying words when the image cannot supply them.
- Carousel: clear argument and progression, strong cover, useful interior content, purposeful rhythm, coherent type/brand and readable copy on every slide.
- Campaign: a memorable common idea, distinct roles/compositions, recognisable brand, consistent assets/typographic system and useful action/information.
- Faithful copy: fidelity of geometry, type, colour, identity and copy separately; originality is not a substitute.

Record: first read; idea/device; up to three problems with image/region and rule IDs; proposed corrections; what changed after viewing the next render. Correct idea before micro-detail. Three correction rounds maximum after the developed initial set; concept replacement counts. Stop early if no consequential observed defect remains, or a concrete tool/asset/permission block prevents correction. At the limit deliver with unresolved defects disclosed.

Self-review is not release acceptance. Technical checks and high rubric averages alone cannot establish human approval or comparative quality. Delivery and publication follow the user's actual quality contract; report any required approval that is still pending and do not fabricate benchmark claims.

Calibration of the scale requires an actual comparable artwork. Record three observed differences and their consequences using `../references/composition-study.md`; do not award 4 because the words fit and no collision was noticed. If no fair comparison is inspectable, visual reference-level quality remains unverified. This comparison does not replace the user's blind release gate.
