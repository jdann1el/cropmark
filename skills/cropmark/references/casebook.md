# Casebook — twelve provenance-backed examples

Six historical internal runs, three externally declared AI projects, three user-selected quality references. Current calibration outputs are excluded from this library. An AI provenance label is not a quality verdict. Original pixels and traces are preserved in the local research package; third-party screenshots are not redistributed in the public skill.

## Evidence limits
Rules R01–R14 are craft hypotheses and observational guidance; this casebook is not a prevalence study or causal trial. R03/R08/R11/R12/R13 have multiple visible examples and counterexamples here; padding, geometric tolerances and tangency thresholds are exercised with didactic fixtures, not established as universal statistical laws. Publication still needs human blind review.
