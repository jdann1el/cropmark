# Typography as composition

Choose faces by the message, imagery and scale, not the sector alone. Start with display, support and metadata roles; one family can fill all three. The bundled OFL fonts are options, not an approved-taste whitelist. A supplied/licensed face may be used when available; keep its file and licence in the editable package and record the font actually rendered.

Compare candidate headlines using the real copy: width, weight, rhythm, counters, punctuation and relation to the image. A condensed face saves width but may reduce warmth; an italic can change voice but does not automatically create editorial quality. Mixed families require a visible relationship. Stable roles across a set matter more than a fixed family count.

Set the main mass confidently, then subordinate supporting copy. Use a small intentional scale; avoid a cluster of almost-equal sizes. For a 1080-wide post, body around 28–36 px is a useful starting point, with display sized for the composition; validate at 360 px. A short label can be smaller if still useful and readable. Required copy governs any suggested word count.

Break lines at meaning seams. Adjust text measure, crop, layout and spacing before shrinking the headline; adjust tracking/leading for the actual face and words rather than applying universal tight settings. Preserve accent/descender clearance. Test the script and punctuation actually used; fallback glyphs can change both style and bounds.

Use declared shared edges and an explicit spacing scale. Containers serving the same role keep equivalent padding; optical adjustments are checked against actual ink, not just CSS boxes. Separate independent blocks clearly; overlaps, cropped letters and staggered baselines need a compositional purpose and must preserve required reading.

Inspect full size and feed: first read, secondary read, breaks, contrast and meaningful small copy. `../references/visual-rules.md` R03–R09 covers common failures and legitimate exceptions.

## Resolve groups and their real edges

Before final spacing, identify the actual groups: headline phrase, premise/answer, brand lockup, caption, offer and action. Choose an anchor for each group (shared left/right edge or a deliberate centre) and a containing region. Do not place independent lines by unrelated coordinates and hope they form a system. Words belonging to one phrase should read as one unit; a pair spread across the canvas needs a visible comparison or subject-label relationship.

Distinguish a fitted text panel from a large colour field. A fitted panel wraps the union of its text ink with the declared inset. A large colour field can use tension and open space, but its content still needs a deliberate anchor; it is not an excuse for a half-empty accidental label. Compare the spaces around the whole group, not just the font's nominal line-height box.

For a centred label or lockup, compare left/right and top/bottom visible gaps; start with balanced pairs. Apply a small optical correction when actual letter shapes require it and verify in the render. For a left-aligned editorial block, equal horizontal padding is not promised, but its vertical padding and relationship to the next block still are. Do not centre every composition: asymmetry and bleed are valid when the relationship is controlled.

Keep semantically linked premise/answer copy closer than unrelated groups unless a deliberate image or narrative break separates them. If “Si una nota basta,” floats far from its answer, move or resize the pair before adding a divider. Use a compact spacing scale and actual ink bounds; no fixed pixel or percentage value is a universal quality law.

Expressiveness can come from width, weight, italic voice, a cropped/rotated large word, colour-field shape, a purposeful asterisk or repeated slash rhythm. Choose one coherent language that serves this subject. Do not replace every thin rule with a different stock decoration. Remove filler while retaining the contrast and substance that gave the piece character.

For a quiet photo-led campaign, a useful initial wordmark size is about 2–3% of canvas width and supporting information around 2.5–3.5%; use actual ink and feed reading to adjust. This is a starting point for subordinate identity, not a ban on large brand-led typography. Avoid giving hours or minor support the weight of a headline without a visible reason. A strong minimal composition can have confident display type and much quieter supporting voices.

Before delivery, declare fitted panel/group bounds and promised axes/gaps in the layout contract and run the geometric checker. Use balanced vertical padding for a fitted panel unless a visible asymmetric relation is intentional; use a maximum gap where closely related copy must remain grouped. Keep the raster review too: correct metadata does not prove optical quality.
