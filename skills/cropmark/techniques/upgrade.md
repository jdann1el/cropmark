# Upgrade an existing piece

Name the observed problem and preserve required topic, copy, identity, brand and count. A narrow correction follows the short route: change the relevant relationships and inspect the result. For a substantial redesign, use `../references/art-direction.md` to explore concepts; the old layout is evidence, not a compulsory template.

Distinguish an identity-critical source photo from an illustrative design reference. Real people, exact products and supplied marks stay faithful when required. An illustrative low-resolution mock-up can be replaced by a disclosed conceptual asset when the brief permits it. Asset changes must not silently change facts or claim product accuracy.

Refine idea and composition before small details. A structural change is allowed; do not cap the work at three cosmetic changes. Keep the actual input as the before image. Any fabricated bad original used for teaching is explicitly labelled and excluded from performance evidence. Do not claim a new piece wins until it has been compared under the agreed protocol.
