# Carousel

Default: 1080×1350 per slide, consistent ratio throughout, feed preview 360 px wide. Follow the requested slide count; when unspecified choose the shortest sequence that delivers the argument. Body around 28–36 px and generous consistent margins are starting points, verified in feed renders.

Read `../techniques/carousel-system.md`. Create a sequence of claims and visual evidence, rather than splitting an article into equal boxes. Roles may be hook, problem, contrast, example, proof, practical step and close. Merge roles when the brief is short. Typography alone may carry a complete sequence.

Each slide has a first read, enough content for its role, and a relationship to its neighbours. Keep palette, type roles, and one or two identity cues continuous, while varying scale, vertical position, crop, and image/type relation where progression benefits. A swipe cue needs a specific reason, such as a reveal or continuation across frames; do not add one by habit. No mandatory every-third-slide breather, anchor percentage, serif/sans stance, divider, counter, or decorative category row. Keep those that serve information or identity. These are candidate rules pending validation.

Make the headline the dominant type mass unless the brief names another first read. At a 360 px-wide feed preview, record visible cap heights and target a headline at least 2× the required small-copy cap height; when the brief names a different first read, record and compare that hierarchy instead. Required small copy should remain at least 14 px high and readable. Image and headline may interact, but neither may cover a key object detail or any required copy; no text or detail may be cut off accidentally. Preserve the local contrast targets in R16. Use a CTA at most once, on the closing slide when appropriate; a fine underline or arrow can mark it, while repeated filled pills are not the default. Candidate guidance pending validation.

Required wording and factual nuance survive. Shorten only permitted text while preserving meaning; propose a changed count if a real conflict blocks readability. A reference copy keeps its supplied structure. Render and inspect the full sequence, each individual slide and a feed contact sheet; `../references/visual-rules.md` governs finishing.
