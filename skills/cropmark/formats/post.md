# Post / ad

Default master: 1080×1350 (4:5), feed preview 360 px wide. Keep essential content inside the platform's safe area; `qa.py` uses 64 px as the post margin starting point. Other requested dimensions govern the deliverable. Around 8 headline words and 25 support words are editing prompts, not permission to remove required copy.

A post has one primary communication job: reveal, explain, compare, announce or invite. Choose a visual relationship that carries it: image and type sharing an object, a revealing crop, scale contrast, brand shape as a mask, a documentary scene, an expressive typographic statement. Use the actual content to decide; no particular relationship is inherently premium.

Compose with the available imagery. A quiet zone can be photographed, cropped or supplied by a genuine information panel. Let type, framing and brand assets participate in the idea. Labels, CTAs and effects have their function and necessary scale; there is no mandatory masthead, tiny spec row, script word or footer.

For an ad, preserve the recognisable product and required identity; use only supplied offers, claims and facts. For information that cannot be pictured literally, use an honest relevant situation or a typographic concept, without fabricating a result screen or demonstration. Apply `../references/visual-rules.md` and review both full size and feed.

When adapting a carousel idea to a single post or flyer, retain its specific object/gesture and identity cues, then recompose the hierarchy for the single frame. Let the headline lead unless the brief names another first read. At a 360 px-wide feed preview, target a headline cap height at least 2× required small-copy cap height; where the brief names another first read, record and compare that hierarchy. Keep required small copy at least 14 px high, readable, and all key object detail and required copy visible. Use the R16 local-contrast targets. These transfer rules are candidates pending validation.
