# Format: single post / ad (1080x1350, 4:5)
Spec: 1080x1350. Keep key content in the central 1012 px (IG grid shows 3:4). Margins 72-96 px sides, 80-112 top, 80-120 bottom. Feed test at 360 px wide. Headline ≤8 words; body ≤25 words; quote posts ≤15 words total. Min type at 1080 wide: body 28 px, meta 20 px.

## What separates portfolio posts from templates
1. **One visual idea** (an object-metaphor, a product in heroic light, a person in a moment, a brand shape) — never "nice stock photo + headline on top".
2. **The brand acts as a graphic**: colour field, ribbon/swoosh, symbol used as mask, mascot. It may leave the canvas.
3. **Type contrast inside the headline**: heavy condensed caps + italic serif lowercase, or one giant keyword broken by syllable and overlapped by the subject. Small text stays tiny and calm (20-24 px caps, tracked).
4. **Quiet zone chosen in the photo** (sky, wall, colour field). If the photo has none, change layout (colour block + cutout) instead of adding a dark box.
5. **System marks**: handle/logo small in the same spot on every post; optional masthead line (name · role · ID) and footer line.

## News that can't be pictured (opening hours, online booking, an event day)
Show the *moment the benefit happens* instead of the venue: phone in hand with an in-scene confirmation card ("booked · Thu 18:00"), the empty chair waiting, the calendar day circled. One in-scene element is allowed for this; keep it smaller than the hero.

## Layout patterns
- **Colour field + cutout**: flat brand colour, product/person cut out at 50-70% height, giant word behind or broken around it.
- **Full-bleed photo + script accent** (luxury, real estate, hospitality): photo owns the canvas, serif caps headline with one word in script, thin spec row with icons, small badge, CTA bar at the bottom.
- **Object metaphor**: one everyday object on solid colour or textured paper carries the message; text printed *on* or beside the object.
- **Product hero dark**: gradient navy/purple/black, product with rim light & reflection, model name large, 3-4 tiny spec labels with hairlines.
- **Type-only statement**: colour field + 1 sentence set big with rhythm (staircase indent, mixed weights) + masthead/footer.
- **Promo (mid-tier)**: saturated colours, product big, badge (OFF, NEW), CTA. Fine for local retail; never call it premium.

## Ad-specific
- Product must be recognisable at 360 px; logo legible but small.
- Claims only from the brief; no "clinically proven", no invented % or ratings.
- CTA as a small pill or line, not a giant button, unless the brief is pure performance.
