# Style: horror-mystery
Formats: thumbnail, post
Best for: horror stories, true crime, mysteries, paranormal, suspense

## Signature
One impossible detail in an ordinary place (the only lit house in a blackout, a figure in a window, a door open that shouldn't be). Single light source, deep blue-black grade, warm accent only on the anomaly. Zero to three words; if any, small serif or distressed caps placed in the dark zone, never over the anomaly.

## Recipe
Prompt: 'cinematic night photo, [ordinary place], [one anomaly], single light source, deep shadows, fog, blue-black grade, warm light only on [anomaly], wide shot with empty dark sky, no text'. Add vignette + slight grain in the engine. Fonts: DMSerifDisplay or Anton at small size; optional 'part 1' pill for series.

## Avoid
Gore, jump-scare faces, red blood text, several anomalies.
