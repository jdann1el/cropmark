# Style: cinematic-editorial
Formats: post, carousel
Best for: Production companies, photographers, fashion, culture, premium services

## Signature
B/W or warm film still, white thin headline justified across the width (word spacing stretched) with one italic serif word; micro labels in corners (studio, section, page) forming a grid.

## Recipe
Fonts: InstrumentSans Regular 64-80 px justified + InstrumentSerif Italic for the accent word; corner labels 16-18 px caps.

## Avoid
Bold heavy type; centred text.
