# Style: cinematic-editorial
Formats: post, carousel
Best for: Production companies, photographers, fashion, culture, premium services

## Signature
B/W or warm film still, white thin headline justified across the width (word spacing stretched) with one italic serif word; micro labels in corners (studio, section, page) forming a grid.

## Recipe
Fonts: InstrumentSans Regular 64-80 px justified + InstrumentSerif Italic for the accent word; corner labels 16-18 px caps.

## Avoid
Bold heavy type; centred text.

## Culture and event announcements
The film-still signature needs a specific connection to the event: use an appropriate supplied scene, recognisable prop or a deliberately drawn topic cue, with the required title/schedule/CTA readable together. A large abstract shape and tiny corner copy alone can become an interchangeable poster. If the brief explicitly requests typography-only or faithful copying, preserve that structure and communicate the event through type instead of introducing a photo.
