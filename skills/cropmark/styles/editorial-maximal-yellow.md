# Style: editorial-maximal-yellow
Formats: post, carousel
Best for: Agencies, studios, creators, personal brands, opinion content

## Signature
Lemon yellow + black + cream paper. Headline mixes heavy condensed caps (Anton/BigShoulders) with italic serif lowercase (InstrumentSerif/DMSerifDisplay Italic) in one tight block. Objects photographed as metaphors (floppy disk, air freshener, sticky notes, sandwich board) with text printed on them; halftone B/W cutouts (ears, mouths, portraits).

## Recipe
Copy is conversational and split across slides. Halftone: grayscale → threshold dots in the engine. Handle @ small in the same corner. Leading .9, sizes wildly contrasted (220 px caps vs 60 px italic).

## Avoid
Pastel colours; tidy symmetric grids; more than 3 type sizes in a block.
