# Style: seamless-panorama
Formats: carousel (single-post variant: one panel with the cutout bleeding off one edge, same type system — good for nutrition/health posts)
Best for: Health, nutrition, education, tutorials, lists

## Signature
All slides designed as one wide canvas then sliced; cutout photos (plates, plants, objects) cross the borders. Olive/sage + cream (or brand pair); serif italic titles, small sans body, pill labels for sub-heads, centred '→' at the bottom, brand name tiny at top. Last slide: person photo + save CTA.

## Recipe
Build width = N×1080 in HTML, slice with clip. Fonts: DMSerifDisplay Italic or InstrumentSerif Italic titles 64-90 px, InstrumentSans 28-32 px body, pills 24 px on accent.

## Avoid
Elements cut exactly at a border with nothing on the next slide; tiny unreadable body.
