# Style: food-hero
Formats: thumbnail, post
Best for: cooking channels, recipes, chefs, food reviews

## Signature
The hero dish huge (50-60 % of frame), hands or a person in action (stirring, serving, tasting), steam/sizzle, warm side light. Text as a handwritten note, recipe card or tape label ("la de mi abuela"), or 1-2 bold words naming the stake (time, place, person).

## Recipe
Prompt: 'overhead-angle or 45° food photo, [dish] filling the frame, [hands/person action], steam, rustic surface, warm window light, shallow depth, quiet area [left/top], no text'. Text: cm.sticky with Caveat-Variable (weight 700) or a white card. Grade warm (+8 warmth), saturation 1.15.

## Avoid
Empty plates, cold light, more than one dish competing, listing ingredients on a thumbnail.
