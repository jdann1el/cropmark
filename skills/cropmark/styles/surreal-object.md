# Style: surreal-object
Formats: post, thumbnail
Best for: Tech, brands, campaigns, concept posts, awareness

## Signature
One everyday object transformed into a metaphor (a mouse with bandages, a lock made of ice) on a solid intense colour; lots of air; tiny headline in a corner.

## Recipe
Prompt: 'conceptual product photo, [object] [transformation], centred, solid [colour] background, soft studio shadow, hyperreal, no text'.

## Avoid
Two metaphors; explanatory text.
