# Development calibration and repairs

The first anonymous human calibration is recorded without substituting later attempts. Three briefs were run in no-skill, frozen rc5 and art-direction-rc1 conditions, with low/high effort requested equally: 18 runs, 48 PNGs.

The initial candidate therefore failed calibration: 0/3 acceptable at low effort, 1/3 at high effort. D3-low acceptance marks are not converted into an inferred preference. These development data do not establish held-out superiority.

The human notes favoured stronger typographic character while identifying disconnected phrase parts, unclear axes, unequal padding, automatic brand strips and overly prominent brand/support text. This informed observable rules rather than a universal font, colour or ornament ban.

Six new preview5 development runs preserved the same supplied resources and wording. A separate reviewer found a real note panel with 65/181px vertical insets and a 101px premise/answer gap; another campaign still used near-equal display/support sizes and a prominent wordmark. Those renders remain preserved.

The geometry checker now supports balanced padding pairs, reasoned optical offsets, containment and maximum gaps. Fitted panels and promised axes/gaps require a declared check. The actual unmodified panel fails the new balance and proximity contract. A development correction produces 65/65px insets and a 36px gap; the same contract passes. Campaign refinement reduces its subordinate wordmark to 30 px, support to 64 px against 112 px display and hours to 32 px regular. Original files remain unchanged. These are coordinator-guided development corrections, not independent blind wins or replacements for the initial calibration.

Process tests also exposed batching before representative inspection and omission of a pre-render concept record. The skill now requires a compact direction record before rendering, actual two-sketch inspection and representative-only rendering before extension. Failed process tests are retained. The six preview5 runs followed that sequence. The record is not proof of aesthetic quality; only actual images and human review can establish that.
