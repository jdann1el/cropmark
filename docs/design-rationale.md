# Design rationale: adapting an art-direction workflow to social graphics

The study source is the locally installed `design-taste-frontend` v2 SKILL.md and its described workflow (inspected 19 October 2026). Its own 247-site research and reported blind tests are author claims; they are not Cropmark evidence. No taste-skill runtime, source code or gallery library is bundled here. Cropmark's reference analyses and engine contracts are its own resources.

| Useful mechanism from the source | Cropmark implementation | Why the adaptation matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inventory the actual subject before styling | Five to ten concrete objects, rituals, constraints, places or contrasts; three subject-specific concept sentences | A niche label alone does not supply a story for a thumbnail or campaign. |
| Compare directions rather than choose the category default | Two rendered compositions with real headline length and available assets | A random type/palette deal cannot establish composition or message fit. |
| Ground an idea in available imagery | Check actual assets and capabilities; preserve real subjects; use generated imagery for conceptual scenes | Product identity and truth of an implied before/after matter in social advertising. |
| Inspect a real render, correct, inspect again | Full-size plus feed review; preserve attempts; three-round ceiling including concept replacement | Small format hierarchy and image relationships must survive a real feed. |
| Mechanically inspect observable defaults | Optional declared margin/alignment/padding/gap/token checks | Raster geometry can be checked; visual purpose, optical alignment and originality remain visual judgments. |
| Removing ornaments alone can leave a weak piece | Rule R14 checks the remaining idea and message | A clean composition can still be interchangeable or empty. |

Cropmark deliberately does not inherit a universal no-ornament house style or frontend breakpoints as design laws. Fine rules, small labels, serifs, gradients, asymmetry and overlap are legitimate where their information, identity or compositional role is visible. A written justification does not excuse an unresolved collision.

Social graphics require additional modules: truthful visual stakes and duration-safe composition for thumbnails; sequence and varying narrative roles for carousels; invariant identity with different jobs for campaign pieces; faithful reconstruction when novelty would violate the task. Short routes avoid imposing concept exploration on a narrow approved edit.

These differences are workflow hypotheses. They do not establish that Cropmark is better than taste-skill or meets a professional portfolio standard. The approved calibration and held-out human gates decide the tested scope of that claim.
