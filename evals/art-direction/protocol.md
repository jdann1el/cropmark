# Art-direction candidate — evaluation contract

Status: development/calibration, not accepted for publication. rc5 remains an unaccepted historical candidate. This protocol supersedes only the creative-update experiment, not prior evidence.

## Fixed conditions
Three fictional development briefs (D1 thumbnail, D2 four-slide typographic carousel, D3 three-post coffee campaign), three conditions (no design skill / frozen rc5 / frozen art-direction candidate), GPT-6.1 Sol low. A separate high-effort sample repeats all three conditions on the same development briefs. Fresh independent native Codex subagents, no external model APIs. Common tools: files/terminal, neutral Pillow/HTML render mechanics, same fonts and source imagery, image viewer; no network, generation or unrelated skills inside runs. Two native imagegen assets are generated once by the coordinator and shared byte-identically. This experiment tests composition/type/campaign workflow with supplied imagery; autonomous imagery sourcing/generation is untested.

Common ceiling is initial developed set plus at most three corrections; rc5 keeps its own tighter stop rule. That difference is part of the workflow treatment, and must be reported. Candidate exploration sketches are process artifacts. Preserve every attempt, output, commands, reads, elapsed timing when exposed, and actual model/effort when exposed. Do not invent unavailable token counts or seeds. Parallel elapsed work is not wall duration.

Every run delivers exact PNG count/size/copy, feed views, an editable package, observed review/correction notes, copied-directory rerender and a localized text-edit check. A fresh copied package establishes portability within the tested runtime, not across all OS/render versions. A single result per condition is practical calibration, not statistical evidence.

## Human calibration gate
Present anonymous A/B/C results separately for low and high effort. Randomize labels per case/effort. Keep the mapping outside the blind gallery. Ask the human for preference, acceptability against references and concrete defects; allow tie/none. Automated critiques and self-ratings cannot fill human votes. The three educational before/after pairs are labelled teaching comparisons and are not blind results.

## Held-out phase
After human calibration and any revisions, freeze/hash the candidate. Author six new briefs then, two per family, with supplied real-photo identity, conceptual composition and existing-brand constraints represented. Supply a common offline reference-image study bank and identical source assets/font availability to all three conditions. The study bank is for looking, not for copying third-party assets into output. This held-out capability is broader than the initial text-atlas-only calibration and is not pooled with its counts. Freeze input hashes and dispatch exact isolated brief files, never a shared multi-brief list. Their exact briefs must not be used to revise the candidate. Run the three conditions once per brief (18 runs). Do not rerun to seek wins. Consumed or revealed briefs become development material for a later candidate.

Publication requires complete technically sound editable deliveries; human blind preference over each comparator on at least 5/6 cases (ties not wins); reference-quality approval on at least 5/6; every family has an approved result. Record losses, ties, defects and cost limits. This is an agreed practical gate, not a statistical or cross-model equivalence claim.

## Portability and coverage
The skill has no mandatory JEV/provider/model dependency. Adapt to capabilities and explicit budget; record unsupported image inspection or missing assets. Cross-model quality is untested until measured. High effort is a ceiling sample, not proof that lower effort failed. The renderer/strict QA regression suite is separate from all visual acceptance.
