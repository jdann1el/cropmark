## Capability contract to freeze before dispatch

Preserve required editable text, logos and composition. Shared photographs remain the final source of required identity/material. Final outputs must not silently substitute generated identity or flatten all required text into a generated image.

The historical experiment prohibited generation within runs. This recalibration expands **all three conditions equally** to at most2 native image-generation calls per run for visual guides only; no new final visual assets or claims. Usage is optional, same ceiling for every condition. Guides, exact prompts and generated files must be preserved; final text/shape/source-photo layers rebuilt editably. This expanded capability is a different experiment and is not pooled with historical counts. Never change the contract mid-run or give only the candidate extra tools. A failed generation call counts toward the ceiling; native-tool unavailability is recorded, with no provider fallback.

No network, unrelated skills, JEV, installation, additional model APIs or persistent account changes inside runs. No delegation within production runs: the candidate uses its documented self-review fallback. Coordinator technical verification occurs after completion and does not guide aesthetic revisions. This tests a self-review capability setting, not the effect of independent critic delegation. Any mandatory memory recall must remain generic and non-sensitive; prior case/results/ballots are excluded from evaluation context.

Common ceiling: one developed result plus at most3 corrections for the entire requested set, concept changes included. Frozen rc5 keeps its own tighter2-round limit; that workflow difference remains part of the treatment. Process sketches are not extra finals. No attempt replacement to seek a preferred result; record technical failure, incomplete stage or exhausted limit. A stopped/incomplete calibration cannot become PASS by dropping its difficult case.

## Verification and review

Every run delivers exact count/dimensions/copy; final feed views; reproducible source, source images, masks/transforms where applicable, actual fonts/licenses; compact observed review/round log. Coordinator verifies each package outside the producer directory and a localized text edit. Strict metadata QA/geometry stay separate from actual pixel reading, applied identity and aesthetic quality.

Randomize anonymous A/B/C independently per case/effort; keep mapping outside the blind page. Preserve all cases including losses, ties and none. Human preference and reference-level judgment determine calibration; critic/self-scores cannot fill votes. This practical calibration is not statistical superiority or cross-model equivalence.

Only after human calibration and resulting revisions may a new candidate be frozen and six genuinely new held-out briefs authored. H1–H6 and all N/P development cases remain consumed/development. The original final gate remains: complete technically correct editable deliveries, at least5/6 blind wins against each comparator, at least5/6 reference-quality approvals and an approved result in every family. No publication, merge or installation replacement before that gate.
